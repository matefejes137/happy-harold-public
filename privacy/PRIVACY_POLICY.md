# Happy Harold – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game **Happy Harold** (the "App") handles information.
The App is developed and published by **Blue Stern Consulting Pte. Ltd.**, Singapore ("we", "us"),
under the name Blue Stern Apps.

## Summary

- We do **not** ask you for your name, email address, or any account. There is no sign-up.
- Your game progress (scores, settings, missions, medals) is stored **only on your device**.
- The App shows ads provided by **Google AdMob** and uses **Google Firebase** (Analytics, Crashlytics
  and Remote Config) to understand how the game is played, fix crashes and adjust game settings. These Google services
  may collect certain device information as described below.

## Information stored on your device

The App stores your game data locally on your device: high score, best depth, settings (sound,
haptics, colour-blind mode, reduce effects), tutorial status, missions, medals and rank. If the App
crashes, a technical crash log may be stored on your device to help with troubleshooting. This data
stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may
collect and process information such as:

- your device's advertising ID (e.g. Android Advertising ID), and
- device and app information (device model, operating system, IP address, language, general
  location derived from the IP address), and
- ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped).

Depending on your consent choices and your region, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.
Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Analytics and settings (Google Firebase)

The App uses **Firebase Analytics** to collect anonymous usage events, such as starting and ending a
game, the score and depth reached, and whether an ad was shown. This helps us improve the game. The
App also uses **Firebase Remote Config** to receive game settings (for example, how often ads are
shown). The App uses **Firebase Crashlytics** to send crash reports (a stack trace, device model,
Android version, app version and the game state at the time of the crash, such as the depth
reached) so we can fix bugs. Firebase may collect an app-instance identifier and device information.
Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Your choices and consent

- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required):** when you first open the App
  you are asked for your consent choices through Google's consent form. You can change your choices
  at any time in the App under **Settings → Privacy options**.
- **Ad personalisation:** you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your
  device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
- **Stop all collection:** uninstall the App.

## Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App,
please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. Information collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is
processed by Google under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Data is
transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

## Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App. Data collected by Google services
is retained according to Google's retention policies. You can request deletion of analytics data
associated with your device by contacting us.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this same
address with a new effective date.

## Contact
